Sheridan/GIR remains convicted in his BUY-rating - (link to note): "We continue

unknowns in the AI arena and OMs are not going up as many would have thought 
given their efficiencies. 

Kash/GIR remains BUY-rated - noting (link to note): "ServiceNow's AI 
execution is driving deeper consolidation, expanding AI budget share and 
positioning it to at least meet its FY26 $1B AI ACV target. Revenue contributions 
should scale meaningfully in FY26 and become a prominent growth driver in 
FY27 as usage ramps and Assist packs gain traction. ServiceNow's AI-led 
transformation across front-office CRM, back-office operations, and cross- 
functional domains positions it as a foundational layer in the enterprise AI stack. 
We see the combination of strong execution, expanding product breadth, and 
accelerating AI monetization supporting a durable 20%+ growth trajectory (w/ 
30%+ FCFM's)." [GIR].
