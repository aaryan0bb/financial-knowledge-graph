*Speculative Trading Indicator (Snider): Dur Speculative Trading Indicator 
shows a recent increase in high-risk activity within the US equity 
market. The indicator is constructed basedsolely on trading volumes. 
Specifically, it is calculated as the indexed and re-scaled first principal component 
of monthly changes in the proportion of US public equity dollar trading value 
attributable to [1] unprofitable stocks, [2] penny stocks, and [3] stocks 
EV/sales multiples greater than 10x. During the past month, the share of trading 
activity taking place each of those three groups of stocks has ranked in the top 
quintile of activity since 1990. 

- In particular, recent US equity trading volumes have increasingly centered 
on high-multiple stocks. The median EV/sales multiple of the 50 stocks 
the highest share trading volumes, a simple measure that has historically tracked 
closely with the Speculative Trading Indicator, has alsoBurged during the last few 
months. Today, that EV/sales multiple equals 8x, the highest level in recent 
decades outside of 2000 and 2021. 

- Call option volumes have surged alongside the increase in "speculative" 
cash trading volumes. During the past month, calls   accounted for 61% of 
option volumes. This compares with a 20-year average of 55% and matches 
December 2024 as the highest share in a decade outside of the period from 
summer 2020 through early 2022. 

  In the past, sharp increases in speculative trading activity have signaled 
above-average near-term equity returns but a poor medium-term outlook 
for stocks. We define "sharp increases" as 3-month increases of 15 points or 
more, a threshold reflecting roughly the top 5% of observations since 1990, but 
less than half the magnitude of the rise during the past few months. Theseabove- 
average near-term returns have beenaccompanied by slightly higher near-term 
volatility and, more often than not, have eventually led to market drawdowns. 

- Recent positioning data and market internals indicate the potential for 
these recent rotations to continue. Despite the S&P 500 sittinglat record highs, 
our US equity Sentiment Indicator reflects neutral positioning on average across 
US equity investors. Likewise, after the large increase earlier this year, as of 
week shortEinterest forthe median S&P 500 stock remained above the historical 
average and close to the highest level since As a result of these positioning 
dynamics, market breadth remains extremely narrow, a condition that typically 
signals risk to intra-market momentum.
