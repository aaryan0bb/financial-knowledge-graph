Systematic & Futures Update: Continuation, growing length and some performance improvement 

 1. CTA/trend followers have been continued equity buyers as expected, adding to their long length, 
 which has been overall right-way with price action that hit new ATHs this week in SPX. The vol-based 
 systematic investors - risk parity style and VA vol-control - have been adding in secondary size also, and 
collectively, the total estimated global equity length currently stands at nearly a 7 out of 10 and is 
expected to climb to above an 8 after the next one month's baseline flow. The monthly figure is $80bn, 
 of which $35bn or 44% is expected to be in US markets. The slope or pace of the buying will naturally start 
to flatten after the next week but remains upward sloping currently (Chart 1 below). This positioning has 
helped CTA performance claw back a subset of its YTD losses, where NEIXCTAT is still down a 
 meaningful 8% on the year vs. more than 12% at the lows in May. We estimate that this positive recent 
performance has been led by the equities bucket, while other asset classes have generally been more 
 mixed or challenged. Separately liquidity remains at its cyclically improved levels given the lower 
 volatility notwithstanding lower summer volumes, and SPX gamma hedging flows remain overall pro- 
 cyclical and risk-positive with buy estimates considerably bigger on equivalent up/down spot moves 
 currently. 

 2. In the industry data overall, institutional and total non-dealer US equity length remained at or below 
its 1-yr average but a higher 71-76% historical rank on a min-max scale. Institutional UST length has come 
 a little off its record highs in recent weeks, including $25mm/bp or 1.7x std deviation of sales this week but 
 remains at historically high levels, and with "non-commercials" or specs meaningfully net short. Overall 
 non-dealer dollar flows remained modest-sized in FX - the last time we saw a > 1x std deviation week 
 was back in April - and length at 1-yr lows but not a historical max short (24% current historical rank). 
And in commodities, energies were small-moderately net sold by money managers while gold saw a 
 sizable near $10bn of buying, 2x std deviation flow and the biggest nominal buy week in more than a 
year, to take net contract length to a 42% 1-yr rank and 74% normalized historical rank. We expect the 
 systematic CTA/trend following buying of gold to continue, from $1.7bn in the last one week to $2.1 in 
the next week and $6.7bn in the next one month cumulative in the baseline scenario.
