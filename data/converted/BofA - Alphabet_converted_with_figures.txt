2Q Summary and thoughts on the stock 
Google reported a strong 2Q vs street estimates, with relatively stable search growth (a 
1 pt ex-FX decel. likely due to tough y/y financial services comps) and YouTube and Cloud 
growth acceleration. Margins continued to show strong growth for both core (ex-1x 
charge) and Cloud, and GAAP EPS was up 22% y/y. Given competitive concerns (on 
ongoing user ramp at Open Al), a key positive from print, in our view, was data points 
suggesting stable search + incremental Al user growth for Google. These include: 1) Q/Q 
acceleration in paid click growth to 4% y/y (approaching the 5% growth last year), 2) 
Relatively steady search growth at 11%E (ex-FX) vs 12%E in 1Q (deceleration likely due 
to tough FS comps), & 3) Ramp in Gemini MAUs by 50mn (to 450mn) in 2 months, & 
these users are incremental to search. We think data points that suggest the search/Al 
market is rapidly expanding, and that Google usage can be an Al beneficiary despite 
competitive traction, could help the stock re-rate higher with valuation at 19x '26 GAAP 
(12x core business in sum of parts). 

Four key drivers for the stock (revenue trends, y/y core margin trends, use of capital and 
new disclosures) were mixed in 2Q. 

        Mixed Revenue Trajectory: Total ex-FX gross revenue growth decelerated 
         1 pts q/q to 13% from 14% in 1Q. Search revenues grew 12% y/y (vs Street 
        9%), accelerating 2pts on stable y/y comp, but we estimate ex-FX growth 
        decelerated 1pts q/q on 1pts tougher y/y comp. YouTube grew 13% y/y (vs 
         Street 11%) accelerated 3pts q/q on 8pts easier comp, and Cloud revenues 
        grew 32% (vs Street 27%) accelerating 4pts q/q on a 1pt tougher comp. 

         Strong Margin Trajectory: Core Google operating margins declined 17bps y/y 
        to 38.3%, with core services margin down 8bps. However, excluding a 1x 
         $1.4bn legal charge, margins were up 161bps y/y to 41.8%. Cloud margin 
        improved 942bps y/y to 20.7%. Total headcount at 187.1K was slightly below 
        Street at 187.3K and was up 4% y/y (up 1% q/q). 

         Capital Allocation reflected q/q decline in buybacks: Alphabet bought back 
        $13.6bn of stock in 2Q (vs $15.1bn in 1Q), total $59.5bn in TTM. The cash 
        dividend remained changed at $0.21 per share.

2Q Positives: 
      2Q Rev/EPS beat: 2Q net revenue of $81.7bn and GAAP EPS at $2.31 was 
      above Street estimates at $79.9bn and $2.18 respectively. Revenue upside was 
      driven by strength across all key segments and EPS benefited from higher 
      topline growth. 

      Search revenue beat: 2Q'25 Search revenue at $54.2bn (up 12% y/y) was 
      above Street at $53.0bn (up 9% y/y). 2Q Search growth was driven by strength 
      in the retail and financial services vertical. 

      SPD revenues above Street: Subscriptions, platforms, and devices revenue at 
      $11.2bn (up 20% y/y) was above Street estimates at $10.7bn (up 15% y/y). 
      Management noted growth was led by YouTube subscription followed by 
      Google One, with strong traction for Gemini capabilities. 

      Cloud revenue beat and acceleration: 2Q cloud revenue at $13.6bn (up 32% 
      y/y) was above Street at $13.1bn (up 27% y/y). Google Cloud operating income 
      increased to $2.8bn and operating margin increased to 20.7% (from 11.3% in 
      2Q'24). 
   . Strong cloud deal activity: Management noted strong momentum in Cloud 
      ($250mn deals up 2x y/y in 2Q, backlog up 38% y/y, and new customers +28% 
      q/q) & raised FY25 capex guide to $85bn (vs $75bn prior) to support 
      accelerated data center buildout. 

        Search feature driving higher engagement: Management highlighted Al 
      Overviews are now driving over 10% more queries globally for the types of 
      queries that show them, and this growth continues to increase over time. They 
      also noted strong momentum in multimodal search, fueled by the combined use 
      of Lens, Circle to Search, and Al Overviews. 

      Growing Gemini app traction: Alphabet highlighted Gemini app now has over 
      450mn monthly active users (vs 400mn in May), with daily requests growing 
      over 50% since 1Q. 

      Al integrations improving Search performance: Management highlighted 
      advertisers using Al Max in Search campaigns typically see 14% more

      conversions on average.

Read-across for Online Media & Amazon AWS 
Meta - Solid YouTube & Search 2Q results a positive read across 
We view Google's ad revenue beat as a positive read-through for Meta and believe 
YouTube's performance offers a more relevant comparison for Meta's ad trends than 
Google Search. YouTube revenues grew 13% y/y in 2Q (vs Street 11%) and had a modest 
3pt q/q acceleration in 2Q (on an 8pt easier comp). Ex-FX the growth we estimate that 
growth was stable in 2Q at around 12%. Management highlighted that YouTube growth 
in 2Q was driven by DR followed by brand advertising. 

For 2Q'25, we estimate stable q/q advertising revenue growth for Meta at $44.5bn (up 
 16% y/y) vs Street $43.7bn. However, ex-FX this represents a 5pt q/q deceleration, 
while YouTube's ex-FX growth was stable. We think Meta could report total revenue at 
or above the upper end of its 2Q revenue guidance range (which was $45.5bn). We 
would expect the upper end of 3Q revenue guidance to be $1bn higher than 2Q reported 
revenues. 

Network rev. & YouTube beat, modest positive read across for SMID platforms 
We think Google network ($7.4bn vs Street $7.2bn) and YouTube beat ($9.8bn vs Street 
$9.5bn) are a slightly positive read across for SMID platforms (Pinterest, Snap), as 
results suggest a stable ad environment (vs significant uncertainty in April). In 2Q, 
Google network revenue growth accelerated 1pts to down 1% y/y on a 4pt easier comp, 
while YouTube growth was relative stable ex-FX at 12%E. 

Pinterest: For 2Q'25, we expect Pinterest revenue growth to decelerate 1pt q/q to 15% 
y/y on a 2pt more difficult comp, with revenues of $980mn (vs Street $974mn, up 14% 
y/y). As Google's results suggest a stable ad market with 1Q, we continue to anticipate 
modest upside potential to Street 2Q estimates. 

Potential factors that could drive upside in 2Q include: Recovery in CPG category, 
lapping F&B headwind comps, ramping traction of new DR solutions (Performance+, 
Dynamic creative optimization, ROAS ads bidding) and growing 3P partnerships. Risk is 
sluggish recovery in CPG vertical, potential pressure on brand ad spend due to macro 
uncertainty, slower than expected ramp of new DR solutions & 3P partnerships. 

Snap: For 2Q'25, we expect Snap ad revenue growth to decelerate 6pts q/q to 3% y/y 
on 6pts easier comp, with ad revenues of $1,162mn (vs Street $1,186mn, up 5% y/y).

Price objective basis & risk 

Alphabet (GOOGL / GOOG) 
Our price objective of $217/$217 is based on 19x 2026E core Google GAAP EPS plus 
cash per share. Alphabet has traded at an average multiple of 22x GAAP P/E over the last 
ten years and we think our multiple is reasonable vs history given expectations for 
double-digit revenue growth, Cloud margin expansion, and opportunity to capitalize on 
strong Al assets. 

Downside risks are: 1) loss of search traffic to Al tools from competitors, 2) LLM 
integration in search may take longer than expected or negatively impact search 
revenues, 3) revenue pressure from compliance with the EU Digital Markets Act (DMA), 
4) adverse judge ruling on the ongoing Department of Justice (DOJ) search distribution 
trial, and 5) potential for increasing Capex and lower FCF-given Al investments.
