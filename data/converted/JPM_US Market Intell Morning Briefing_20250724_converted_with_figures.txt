JPM MARKET INTEL EQUITY & MACRO NARRATIVE 
Another day, another record high. With the US / Japan deal announced and the US / EU deal on deck it 
feels like not even a soft earnings print from a major company can derail this bullish run. That said, 
today we get a look at the next batch of meaningful macro data ahead of next Friday's NFP print. While 
Bullishness is not yet consensus, client conversation reveal that even those that skewed bearish are 
throwing in the towel. The combination of the Momentum Unwind and Meme Mania 2.0 are reducing 
(eliminating?) people's ability to hold alpha shorts. Combine that with positive comments on M&A 
pipeline and the impending buyback bid and the storm clouds appear to be lifting ahead of the 
previously feared Aug 1 deadline. Now, if that US / EU hits the tape, we get a deal from China next 
week, and the macro data holds up and look for this market to take a significant step higher. If we had 
this conversation a month ago (or even last week) this would seem like a tail-risk, now it feels like this is 
the base case.

NIKOS: Where are market liquidity conditions still impaired? His full note is here 

    · Market liquidity conditions have seen notable improvement for US Equities, 10y Bunds and 
       corporate bond ETFs, remain somewhat impaired for Eurostoxx & Nikkei futures and 10y USTs 
       & JGBs, and have deteriorated for bitcoin & Ethereum futures. 

       Why is market vol so low? Despite elevated uncertainty and elevated volatility in economic 
       forecasts and surprises, realized macro volatility (i.e. realized GDP and inflation vol) has been 
       rather subdued since 2023. 
    . In turn, low realized macro volatility helped to induce low market volatility in conjunction with 
       other factors such as retail investors and corporate buybacks continuing to act as a backstop to 

       drawdowns in Equities, as well as economic and financial leverage overall remaining relatively 
       contained and thus far not served to amplify these shocks. 

       Within alternative asset classes, digital assets and hedge funds have been seeing an 
       acceleration of inflows this year, in sharp contrast to the weak fundraising seen in private equity 
       and private credit. 

       Hedge funds have produced positive alpha so far in 2025, for a fifth year in a row
