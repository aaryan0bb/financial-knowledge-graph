Focus remains on earnings post GOOGL (+3.6% in the pre) and TSLA (-6% in the 
pre) yesterday evening. Europe broadly trading higher this morning after a late FT 
story indicated that the US and EU are close to a trade deal. Desk flows yesterday 
illustrated strong demand for Japan, coupled with supply in small-cap/exposures that 
are correlated with the short-leg of momentum. 

Japan was one of the clear outliers in yesterday's price action. We saw buyers across 
EWJ and BBJP, with both products witnessing some of their busiest sessions 
YTD. The space has been trading rich recently, but primary market flows were mostly 
muted yesterday given creation/redemption schedules - we'll be watching the space 
closely the next couple of days.

coupled with the continuation of China demand.
