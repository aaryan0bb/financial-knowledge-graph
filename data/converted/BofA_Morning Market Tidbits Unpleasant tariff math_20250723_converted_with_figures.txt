    Summary: This bar chart, titled "US effective tariffs could climb to 16% or even further," illustrates the historical and projected US effective tariff rates across various scenarios and policy shifts. The X-axis details specific events or periods (e.g., "Pre-election," "US-China deal," "Latest"), while the Y-axis quantifies the effective tariff rate in percentages. Key trends include an initial low of 2.3% "Pre-election," a significant spike to a peak of 20.0% during "Liberation Day," followed by a notable decline to a trough of 10.9% after the "US-China deal." The "Latest" effective tariff rate is shown at 15.6%, with potential future increases from hypothetical sector-specific tariffs on Pharma (2.2%) and Semiconductors (0.9%).

    Explanation: The chart underscores BofA Global Research's analysis that US effective tariffs, currently estimated at 15.6%, are significantly higher than their post-US-China deal trough and are nearing 16%. This projection aligns with the surrounding text, which warns that recent tariff announcements could add 5 percentage points to the effective rate, posing an upside risk to inflation and a downside risk to growth, potentially leading to a "stagflationary shock" and influencing the Federal Reserve's policy decisions.

Market Action 
The Asian equity markets that we cover closed positive overnight. Looking at individual 
markets, the Shanghai Composite was flat, while the Korean Kospi, the Indian Sensex, 
the Hang Seng and the Nikkei rose 0.4%, 0.7%, 1.6% and 3.5% respectively. 

As of 7:00 am ET, aggregate European stocks are up 1.0% and S&P500 futures are up 
0.4%. In the bond markets, US Government Bonds are up 2bp to 4.36% and German 
Bund yields are up 2bp to 2.61%. The dollar index is down $1.44 to $97.46. WTI crude oil 
prices are $0.44 lower at $64.87/bbl, and gold prices are at $3422.67/oz.
