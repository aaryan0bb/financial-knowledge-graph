

SIGNIFICANT INCREASE IN SPECULATIVE TRADING 
FICC and Equities 225 July 2025 | 12:04PM UTC 
Our Speculative Trading Indicator has increased sharply during the last few 
months. The indicator now sits at its highest level on record outside of 1998- 
2001 and 2020-2021. The rise in the indicator reflects the elevated recent share 
of trading volumes in unprofitable stocks, penny stocks, and stocks with elevated 
EV/sales multiples. The most actively traded stocks include most of the Mag 7 
along with companies involved in digital assets and quantum computing. 

The increase in the Speculative Trading Indicator mirrors other signs of 
increased risk appetite within the equity market. Call options have recently 
accounted for 61% of option volumes, the highest share since 2021. First-day IPO 
returns have ballooned, and the $9 billion of SPAC issuance in 2Q 2025 
represented the most active quarter since 1Q 2022. A basket of stocks popular 
with retail traders (GSXURFAV) has rallied by 50% since early April.
