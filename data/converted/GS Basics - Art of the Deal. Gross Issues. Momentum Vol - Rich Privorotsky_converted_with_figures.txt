FICC and Equities | 23 July 2025 | 6:53AM UTC 
Big Japan rally overnight, with NKY futures up over 4%-likely amplified by 
levered product flows. Japanese autos surged double digits after a 15% tariff deal 
caught the market off guard (Polymarket had odds of a Japan deal by Aug 1 at 
~20% ) . In the context of 25% tariffs on Canada and Mexico, think mkt surprised 
Japan auto's securing a more favorable status-even compared to what some U.S. 
automakers pay to import their own supply chains. Think 15% Japan tariffs 
would have been unthinkable just months ago ... now, Japan's stock market is near 
all-time highs ... art of the deal. 

Another consequence: Political uncertainty in Japan -Ishiba now denying that 
he will be stepping down after earlier reports from Yomiuri newspaper. The 
40yr auction went poorly, Long-end yields are pushing higher again. Equities 
globally haven't cared about this      but I remain vigilant. These 
fiscal/structural questions aren't going away ... lot of paper around today 
globally (see below) 

Europe should trade up in sympathy, especially after recent hedges being set 
fearing trade escalation. Expect European autos to open sharply higher on 
this, then I think there will be some debate if this emboldens the US to push for a 
more aggressive deal vs the Europeans to acquiesce given what appears to be a 
global baseline that is creeping higher outside of them. 

Earnings season continues to be mixed. Earnings are still supporting the bid in 
AI-related names, but the bar feels it has been raised. TXN guided to 
something that seemed more gradual and is down high single digits post 
market. SAP cloud booking a touch light of very high expectations and should 
trade lower. Think real mixed bag: SHW/LMT/GM/Nokia/ASM/MSCI/PM all 
degrees of disappointing with a number of guidance cuts mixed in. On the 
flipside things like US homebuilders rallied aggressively led 
by DHI and Pulte (both up double digits) as bookings better on low bar despite 
weaker ASPs (Trump considering removing cap gains for home purchases helps 
too). 

Gross felt disgusting again. GSPUWSHI most short vs least short basket rallied 
+2%. KSS was up over 100% at one point yesterday-some serious meme-era 
echoes. I'm old enough to remember the quant quake of August 2007, and how 
quickly that unwind unfolded. I do think performance issues have crept in over 
the last 6 weeks. Systematic crowd has underperformed fundamental L/S, and 
gross remains elevated vs historical levels, due in part to the sheer proliferation 
of quant-driven strategies. The Japan move won't help-momentum drawdown 
is being exacerbated by shortßqueezes.
