2021, and both cases had poor subsequent returns. However, it is worth noting that the 
meme stock rally in 2021 caused more pain as the squeeze in our pair was even more 
pronounced with an 18% rally over the month and at the time, this was a newer

4) The Resurgence of the S&P 500 Index Inclusion Effect 
       The average stock added to the S&P 500 index has outperformed the equal-weight 
S&P 500 by 4 pp on announcement day since 2021. 74% of the stocks added during this 
period have outperformed the equal-weight index on announcement day. Prior to 2021, the 
S&P 500 index inclusion effect had effectively disappeared on average. Roughly 20 
companies have been added to the S&P 500 each year since 2013. Exhibit 9 includes a list 
of 15 non-S&P 500 stocks that may be candidates for future index inclusion. 
       But performance of index additions depends heavily on whether the stock migrates 
from the S&P 400. The average relative return on announcement day equaled 5.3 pp when 
the stock did not migrate from the S&P 400, compared with -0.4 pp when it did. The 
sharpest outperformance of index additions comes ahead of the announcement. Since 2013, 
the average outperformance of index additions in the three months leading up to 
announcement is 12 pp.
