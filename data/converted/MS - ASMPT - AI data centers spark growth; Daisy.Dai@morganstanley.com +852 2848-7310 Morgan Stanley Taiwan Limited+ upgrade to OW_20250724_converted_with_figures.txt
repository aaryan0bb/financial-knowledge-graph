                                                  stage of this trend as the advocacy by NVIDIA was put forward in only May 2025 (link). 
                                                  We expect demand for Al data centers to drive growth in mainstream tools in the next 
                                                  three years.

    Summary: The chart, "Future GW-scale data center architecture," details a hierarchical power delivery system from an MV AC grid to a serverboard's core. It shows a progressive voltage step-down across Infrastructure, Hall, Row/IT Rack, and Serverboard stages, transitioning from 800V DC to 54V, then 12V/6V. The architecture highlights the extensive integration of advanced semiconductor materials like SiC and GaN for efficient power conversion and the deployment of distributed energy storage (long, medium, short duration) throughout the system.

    Explanation: This power architecture, moving towards an 800V HVDC backbone, is critical for enabling next-generation AI data centers with significantly higher power requirements, such as NVIDIA's 1MW IT racks expected from 2027. This technological shift drives increasing demand for specialized silicon, SiC, and GaN components, directly impacting the need for advanced manufacturing tools like wire bonders and die bonders, which are key offerings from companies like ASMPT, the subject of this research.
