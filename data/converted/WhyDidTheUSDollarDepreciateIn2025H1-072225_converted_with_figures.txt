                                  Conclusions 

   . The dollar has depreciated more in the first half of 2025 than yield differentials would 
      have predicted. 

      Regression models indicate that this was due to the trade war and economic policy 
      uncertainty, including concerns among foreign investors about Section 899 and the 
      Mar-a-Lago Accord. 

      Looking ahead, with Section 899 behind us and the trade war likely to be resolved 
      within the next couple of weeks, the US dollar is expected to appreciate again. 

      Renewed strong appetite for US assets after Liberation Day is also visible in the TIC 
      data for May.
