 1) Speculative trading activity adds fuel to narrow-breadth short squeeze 
      Our new Speculative Trading Indicator shows a recent increase in high-risk 
activity within the US equity market. The indicator is constructed basedsolely on 
 trading volumes. Specifically, it is calculated as the indexed and re-scaled first 
principal component of monthly changes in the proportion of US public equity 
dollar trading value attributable to [1] unprofitable stocks, [2] penny stocks, and 
 [3] stocks with EV/sales multiples greater than 10x. During the past month, the 
 share of trading activity taking place in each of those three groups of stocks has 
ranked in the top quintile of activity since 1990. 
      In particular, recent US equity trading volumes have increasingly centered 
 on high-multiple stocks. The median EV/sales multiple of the 50 stocks with the 
highest share trading volumes, a simple measure that has historically tracked 
 closely with   Speculative Trading Indicator, has alsoßurged during the last 
few months. Today, that EV/sales multiple equals 8x, the highest level in recent 
 decades outside of 2000 and 2021.

anticipated. President Lagarde highlighted the resilience of the economy, argued 
that the net effects of tariffs on inflation are unclear and concluded that the 
Governing Council is well positioned "to wait and see." The communication 
therefore suggests that the Governing Council will likely hold ratesunless the 
outlook deteriorates materially.
