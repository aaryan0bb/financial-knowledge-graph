Last week single stocks collectively were modestly net bought (+0.3 SDs), driven 
by risk unwinds with short covers outpacing long sales (1.5 to 1). Last week's 
notional de-grossing in US Single     - long sales and short covers combined - 
was the largest in 6 months and ranks in the 94th percentile on a 5-year 
lookback. 8 of 11 US sectors saw de-grossing activity, led in notional terms by 
Info Tech (long sales > short covers), Consumer Discretionary (short covers > 
long sales), Energy (long sales > short covers), and Utilities (long sales > short 
covers).
