Powering Al: Why "Time to Power" Has Been a Powerful Alpha Driver 

  Definition of a "time to power" solution: any product that can result in data centers powering up more rapidly 
  than the default approach (file a grid interconnection request) 

     Many US grids now have a significant backlog of requests, and the typical grid connection time for data centers has 
     increased from ~2-3 years to ~5-10 years 

  Powering Al Alpha is driven by a fascinating collision of two worlds 

     Al data centers: facility cost of ~$30m per megawatt, with rapidly depreciating chips 

     A power plant often costs ~$2-3 million per megawatt, with a very long asset life 

  Our "time to power" math suggests that the value of a 1-year time advantage is $3-4m per megawatt, more than the 
  entire cost of most power plants 

. The "de-bottlenecking" transactions we have seen appear to include large premia from the lens of the power 
  world, but the premia appear relatively small from the lens of an Al player 

  Bitcoin conversion: a few transactions at effective power price premia of >300%
