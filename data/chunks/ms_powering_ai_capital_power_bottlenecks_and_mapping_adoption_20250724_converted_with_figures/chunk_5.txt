    Summary: This chart, titled "Global Private Debt Fund AUM", illustrates the growth of private debt fund assets under management (AUM) and their deployment rate from 2008 to 2024. The X-axis represents the years, the left Y-axis indicates Total AUM in billions of dollars (shown by blue bars), and the right Y-axis represents the percentage of AUM deployed (shown by a yellow line). Total AUM has shown robust and consistent growth over the period, accelerating significantly from ~$600B in 2015 to an estimated $1.82 trillion by 2024*. The deployment rate, after peaking around 70% in 2010, saw a gradual decline to a low of 58% in 2016, before steadily recovering to an estimated 71% by 2023-2024*.

    Explanation: The sustained, accelerating growth in Global Private Debt Fund AUM, coupled with high and recovering deployment rates, directly supports the assertion that credit markets, particularly private credit, have ample capacity to meet financing needs. This trend suggests that private capital is increasingly becoming a significant source of funding, aligning with the document's broader narrative about financing large-scale investments like data centers through diverse credit channels.
