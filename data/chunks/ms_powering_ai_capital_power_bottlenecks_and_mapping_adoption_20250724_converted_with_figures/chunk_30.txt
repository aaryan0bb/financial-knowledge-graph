Key Takeaways from Our 4th Al Mapping Exercise 

· Broadening of Al exposure and materiality, reaching multi-trillion-dollar scale: 412 stocks ($8.7 trillion of market 
   cap) have increased their exposure to Al and it is more material to 259 stocks ($8.5 trillion of market cap). We count 
   102 stocks ($2.4 trillion of market cap) that increased in both exposure to and materiality of Al 

. There are clear signs of alpha from Al materiality among companies: this can be seen in relative price 
   performance and earnings revisions 

. This Round, the Big Al "rate of change" winners are the Consumer and Real Estate sectors: Almost 30% of 
   Consumer Durables & Apparel stocks saw an increase in exposure, and ~10% experienced an increase in materiality. 
   The percent of Consumer Durables & Apparel companies now qualifying as Adopters moved from 20% to 44%. 
   Consumer Discretionary Adopters jumped from 32% to 47%. 32% of REITs are now more exposed to Al 

   Financials see significant increases in exposure: Insurance Adopters increased from 48% to 71%, while Financial 
   Services Adopters increased from 66% to 73% 

   Regionally, Asia Pacific is the most notable leader: Al exposure increased by 104 companies (net), and 30 of 
   these stocks also saw an increase in materiality
