    Summary:
    This comparative table presents WULF Compute's three core business models for HPC data centers: Cloud Service Provider (CSP), Colocation - Whitespace & Rack Ready, and Build to Suit. It details key operational and financial metrics, highlighting a clear progression in scale and duration. The "Build to Suit" model, targeting hyperscalers, stands out with significantly lower build costs per MW ($2-4M) compared to CSP ($27-30M), the longest contract terms (15+ years), the highest margins (90%+), and the most attractive valuation range (15-20x EBITDA).

    Explanation:
    The chart illustrates TeraWulf's strategic positioning to capitalize on the high-performance computing market by offering diverse data center solutions. The data suggests that large-scale, long-term "Build to Suit" contracts with hyperscalers offer superior financial performance through lower capital expenditure per MW and higher profitability. This model aligns with the broader narrative of converting Bitcoin mining assets into HPC data centers, potentially leading to highly bankable assets suitable for a "REIT End Game" as discussed on the subsequent page.
