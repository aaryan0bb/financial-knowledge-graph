    Summary:
    This figure presents six line charts illustrating the percentage share of Equity Sector Fund Assets for different sectors over the period 2010 to 2025. The X-axis represents the years, while the Y-axis shows the respective sector's share as a percentage. Key trends include a dominant and accelerating rise in Tech sector's share, reaching over 30% by 2025, and a pronounced long-term decline in Energy sector's share, hitting lows around 3.5% in 2020 before a slight rebound. Health Care & Biotech saw a peak in 2015 around 21% followed by a general decline to under 10% recently. Financials, Industrials, and Telecom sectors show more cyclical patterns, with Industrials and Telecom recently experiencing notable uptrends, while Financials remains volatile but ends around its average.

    Explanation:
    These charts provide insights into shifting investor allocations and sentiment within equity markets, highlighting sector-specific fund flows. The pronounced increase in Tech's share signifies a long-term preference for growth and innovation, whereas the general decline in Energy's share reflects evolving market dynamics and potentially a move away from traditional sectors. Such shifts in fund positioning are crucial for understanding broader market preferences and potential implications for sector-specific asset valuations, indicating where capital is accumulating or withdrawing.
