    Summary: This figure presents six time series charts, each illustrating the percentage share of different asset categories or regions within total assets from 2010 to 2025. Key dimensions include Equity Share, Money Market Fund Assets as Share of Global Mutual Fund Assets, and regional shares for US, EM, China, and EM ex. China. The primary trends reveal a general increase in Equity share since 2020 and a sustained rise in US share since 2014, while Money Market Fund share steadily declined with a notable spike in 2020.     Explanation: These trends highlight a significant repositioning of global fund allocations, favoring equities and particularly the US market, likely reflecting stronger economic performance and investor confidence in developed markets. The decline in money market fund share (excluding the 2020 pandemic spike) suggests a move away from low-yield, safe-haven assets. The persistent underperformance and declining share of EM assets, especially EM ex. China, indicate a divergence in global investment flows, with capital increasingly concentrated in specific regions and asset classes.

The consistent decline in EM and EM ex. China asset shares indicates a long-term shift away from emerging markets, perhaps due to evolving risk perceptions, capital outflow dynamics, or better opportunities elsewhere, though the recent uptick in 2025 suggests a potential shift in sentiment.
