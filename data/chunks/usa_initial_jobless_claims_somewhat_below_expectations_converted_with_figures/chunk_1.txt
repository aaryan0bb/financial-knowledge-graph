MAIN POINTS: 

 1. Initial jobless claims declined 4k to 217k in the week ended July 19, against 
 expectations for a modest increase. The four-week moving average of claims fell by 
 5k to 225k. Claims increased by 5k in Kentucky and 4k in Massachusetts but 
declined by 3k in New York, and 2k in California and Michigan (all state-level data 
seasonally adjusted by GS). Nationwide continuing claims-the number of persons 
receiving benefits through standard programs-rose 4k to 1,955k in the week ended 
July 12 from a downwardly-revised level in the prior week (-5k to 1,951k), in line with 
consensus expectations.
